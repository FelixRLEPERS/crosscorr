- B. p-value precision — CLOSED (2026-10-12).
  paper/main.tex reports "p < 0.0001" (lines 26, 43, 355, 909) with
  n_iter = 10000 (scripts/perm_test_18mo_3band.py:10). Minimum
  attainable p is 1/(n_iter+1) = 1/10001 ~ 1.0e-4, so "p < 0.0001"
  sits on the boundary and is formally imprecise. Two options:
  (i) report "p < 1/10001" in text, or (ii) raise n_iter to 50000
  and re-run. Text fix is cheaper and honest.

Resolution (option ii): raised n_iter to 50000 and applied
add-one (Davison-Hinkley) correction. p = 1/50001 ~ 2.0e-5
(all bands). Significance and point estimates unchanged.
Table 5 pairwise p-values were also affected by the 10k->50k
transition (stochastic shift, not systematic); synced to
current JSON (4-decimal rounding). Significance levels in
Table 5 unchanged.
